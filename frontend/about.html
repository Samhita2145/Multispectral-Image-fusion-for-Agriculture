Multispectral Image Fusion for Agriculture

An AI-based platform for early detection of rice leaf diseases using RGB + Pseudo-NIR image fusion.

Project Overview

Rice crops are highly susceptible to diseases such as Bacterial Leaf Blight, Brown Spot, Leaf Blast, Leaf Scald, and Sheath Blight, which significantly reduce crop yield and quality. Traditional detection relies on manual inspection of RGB images, making early and accurate diagnosis difficult. This project proposes a software-based multispectral image fusion framework — combining RGB and Pseudo-NIR imagery — to improve disease detection accuracy and support timely crop health monitoring, without requiring expensive hardware like UAVs.

Objectives
01

Develop an AI-based platform for early detection of rice leaf diseases.

02

Preprocess RGB rice leaf images and generate Pseudo-NIR images to simulate spectral information.

03

Perform multispectral image fusion to enhance disease-related features.

04

Classify rice leaf diseases using an ML/DL model with improved accuracy.

05

Provide disease insights, treatment recommendations, and downloadable analysis reports.

06

Support fast, near real-time analysis suitable for practical agricultural use.
